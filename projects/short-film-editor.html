Short Film Editor

Dialogue edit, sound design, and mix through picture lock. Script supervisor on set.

Overview

Student short from prep to delivery. Dialogue from boom and lav, sound design and room tone on MOS, mix, master, and music once picture locked. I also script-supervised and cut picture with the director.

Contributions

  • Prepro: location notes for noise, sound design, and music, while director and cinematography did shot list, pacing, and logistics.
  • Chose boom vs lav per line, L/J cuts, assembled a continuous dialogue track from mixed takes.
  • Sound design from onset wilds and library, room tone under MOS, then mix, master, and music after lock.
  • Script supervisor: continuity, extra coverage when there was time, production-sound notes, location wilds at wrap at each place we shot, DIT and dailies.
  • Cut picture with the director (rough, class, final). Credits. Cinematographer did color after lock. Then we shipped it.

Challenges

Mixed boom/lav, MOS, and library into one soundtrack so picture cuts didn’t jump. Mix and music locked after picture froze.

Lessons Learned

Script notes and onset wilds actually got used. Boom vs lav per line, plus room tone under MOS, did more than extra effects.

Role

Sound design / mix. Also script supervisor and picture editor.
