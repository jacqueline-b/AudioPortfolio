Short Film Editor

Dialogue edit, sound design, and mix through picture lock — script supervisor on set so post had notes and wilds to work from.

Overview

Student short from prep to delivery. Post is the point here: boom vs lav dialogue, sound design and room tone on MOS, mix/master and music to lock. Script supervisor and picture cuts with the director supported that soundtrack.

Role

Sound Design / Mix (also script supervisor / picture editor)

Selected contributions

  • Dialogue edit: chose boom vs lav per line, L/J cuts, and assembled a continuous track from mixed takes.
  • Sound design and mix: onset wilds plus library, room tone under MOS, then mix/master and music once picture locked.
  • On set as script supervisor: continuity, extra coverage when there was time, production-sound notes, location wilds at wrap, DIT and dailies — so post had a paper trail.
  • Cut picture with the director (rough, class, final) and handed off to the cinematographer for color after lock.

Media

Challenges

Building a continuous soundtrack from mixed boom/lav, MOS, and library so picture cuts didn’t feel like a jump — then locking mix and music once picture was frozen.

Lessons Learned

Script notes and onset wilds pay off in post. Choosing boom vs lav per line, plus room tone under MOS, did more for “real” scenes than adding extra effects.
